# Support Hours and Availability

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the support channels, operating hours, and regional availability for Taskmoor customer support across all plan tiers.

## 2. Support Channels by Plan

| Plan       | Channels Available                                      |
|------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Free       | Community forum, email (best effort)                    |
| Starter    | Email support                                           |
| Business   | Email and live chat with priority queuing               |
| Enterprise | Email, live chat, phone, and dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM) |

## 3. Standard Business Hours by Region

Taskmoor provides support during the following standard business hours for email and live chat channels:

| Region                     | Business Hours                 | Timezone |
|----------------------------|-------------------------------|----------|
| Americas                   | Monday - Friday, 09:00 - 18:00 | US Eastern (ET) |
| Europe, Middle East, Africa (EMEA) | Monday - Friday, 09:00 - 18:00 | Central European (CET) |
| Asia-Pacific (APAC)        | Monday - Friday, 09:00 - 18:00 | Singapore (SGT) |

Business hours exclude weekends and regional public holidays observed in the primary office location for each region.

## 4. Enterprise 24/7 Support

Enterprise customers with active contracts receive 24/7 support coverage for Urgent and High priority issues, including weekends and public holidays. This coverage is provided through the following mechanisms:

- **During Business Hours:** Support is handled by the regional team in the customer's designated support region.
- **Outside Business Hours:** Support is handled by the on-call engineering and support team. Urgent tickets are routed automatically; High priority tickets may be escalated by the customer through the Enterprise support portal or by contacting their named CSM.
- **Named CSM:** Each Enterprise account is assigned a dedicated Customer Success Manager who serves as the primary point of contact for strategic and operational support during business hours.

Normal and Low priority tickets for Enterprise customers are handled during standard business hours in the customer's designated region.

## 5. Community Forum

The Taskmoor community forum (community.taskmoor.com) is available to all plan tiers and is accessible 24/7. The forum is moderated by Taskmoor staff during Americas business hours (Monday-Friday, 09:00-18:00 ET). Forum responses are provided on a best-effort basis and are not subject to SLA response time targets.

## 6. Holiday Schedule

Taskmoor observes the following public holidays during which standard support hours are suspended for the respective region:

- **Americas:** New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Day.
- **EMEA:** New Year's Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Labour Day (May 1), Christmas Day, Boxing Day (Dec 26).
- **APAC:** New Year's Day, Chinese New Year (2 days), Good Friday, Labour Day (May 1), Christmas Day.

Specific dates for each year are published on the Taskmoor Help Center by December 1 of the preceding year. Enterprise 24/7 coverage for Urgent and High priority issues continues through all holidays without interruption.

## 7. Planned Maintenance Windows

Scheduled maintenance is performed during low-traffic hours to minimize customer impact. Planned maintenance windows are announced at least 48 hours in advance on the Taskmoor status page (status.taskmoor.com) and via email to workspace administrators. Emergency maintenance may occur without advance notice when necessary to address critical security or stability issues.

## 8. Contact

Customers may reach support at support@taskmoor.com, through the in-app Help widget, or via the Taskmoor Help Center at help.taskmoor.com.
