# Privacy Policy Summary

## 1. Purpose

This document provides a summary of Taskmoor's privacy practices. The full Privacy Policy is available at taskmoor.com/privacy. This summary is intended as a reference for support operations and customer inquiries but does not replace the full legal text.

## 2. Data We Collect

Taskmoor collects the following categories of personal data:

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, profile photo, and workspace membership details provided during registration and account management.
- **Usage Data:** Information about how users interact with the platform, including features accessed, actions performed, timestamps, IP addresses, browser type, and device identifiers.
- **Content Data:** Projects, tasks, comments, attachments, and other content that users create and store within Taskmoor workspaces.
- **Billing Data:** Payment method details, billing address, and transaction history. Payment card numbers are processed and stored by our PCI-compliant payment processor and are not stored on Taskmoor systems.
- **Communications:** Records of support inquiries, feedback submissions, and communications with Taskmoor.

## 3. How We Use Data

Taskmoor processes personal data for the following purposes:

- Providing and operating the Taskmoor platform and its features.
- Authenticating users and managing account security.
- Processing payments and managing subscriptions.
- Providing customer support and responding to inquiries.
- Analyzing platform usage to improve features, performance, and reliability.
- Sending service-related notifications, including security alerts and billing reminders.
- Complying with legal obligations and enforcing our Terms of Service.

Taskmoor does not sell personal data to third parties. Taskmoor does not use customer content data to train machine learning models.

## 4. Third-Party Data Sharing

Taskmoor shares personal data with third parties only in the following circumstances:

- **Service Providers:** With sub-processors who assist in operating the platform (hosting, payment processing, email delivery, analytics). All sub-processors are bound by data processing agreements.
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
- **Business Transfers:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with appropriate notice to affected users.
- **With Consent:** When the user has provided explicit consent to share data with a specific third party (such as an integration the user enables).

## 5. User Rights

Depending on applicable jurisdiction, users may exercise the following rights:

- **Access:** Request a copy of personal data processed by Taskmoor (GDPR Art. 15, CCPA).
- **Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate personal data (GDPR Art. 16).
- **Erasure:** Request deletion of personal data (GDPR Art. 17, CCPA right to delete).
- **Portability:** Request personal data in a structured, machine-readable format (GDPR Art. 20).
- **Objection:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 21).
- **Opt-Out of Sale:** California residents may opt out of the sale of personal information. Taskmoor does not sell personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@taskmoor.com. Requests are processed within 30 calendar days.

## 6. Data Security

Taskmoor implements technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), access controls, audit logging, and regular security assessments. Details are available in the Taskmoor Security Whitepaper.

## 7. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact privacy@taskmoor.com. Taskmoor's Data Protection Officer can be reached at dpo@taskmoor.com.
