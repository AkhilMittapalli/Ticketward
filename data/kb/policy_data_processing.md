# Data Processing Agreement Overview

## 1. Purpose

This document provides a summary of Taskmoor's Data Processing Agreement (DPA) as it applies to the processing of customer personal data. The full DPA is available at taskmoor.com/dpa and may be executed by Enterprise customers upon request. Starter and Business customers are covered by the standard DPA incorporated into the Terms of Service.

## 2. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Data Controller:** The customer (workspace owner) determines the purposes and means of processing personal data within their Taskmoor workspace.
- **Data Processor:** Taskmoor processes personal data on behalf of the customer strictly in accordance with the customer's instructions and the terms of the DPA.

Taskmoor does not independently determine the purposes of processing customer content data and acts only as a processor with respect to such data.

## 3. Data Location by Region

Taskmoor hosts customer data in regionally designated data centers to comply with data residency requirements:

| Customer Region   | Primary Data Center Location        | Backup Location          |
|-------------------|-------------------------------------|--------------------------|
| United States (US) | US East (Virginia)                 | US West (Oregon)         |
| European Union (EU) | EU Central (Frankfurt, Germany)   | EU West (Dublin, Ireland) |
| Asia-Pacific (APAC) | APAC Southeast (Singapore)       | APAC East (Tokyo, Japan) |

Data residency is determined by the billing address of the workspace owner at the time of workspace creation. Enterprise customers may specify a preferred data region in their contract. Cross-region data transfer occurs only for backup and disaster recovery purposes and is governed by the safeguards described in Section 5.

## 4. Sub-Processors

Taskmoor engages the following categories of sub-processors to assist in delivering the service:

- **Cloud Infrastructure:** Hosting, compute, and storage services.
- **Payment Processing:** Subscription billing and payment card handling.
- **Email Delivery:** Transactional email and notification delivery.
- **Analytics:** Anonymized and aggregated usage analytics (no customer content data is shared with analytics providers).
- **Support Tooling:** Customer support ticket management and live chat.

A current list of sub-processors with their names, purposes, and data center locations is maintained at taskmoor.com/subprocessors. Taskmoor notifies customers of any changes to the sub-processor list at least 30 days before the new sub-processor begins processing data. Workspace administrators subscribed to the sub-processor notification list receive these updates by email.

Enterprise customers with contractual objection rights may object to a new sub-processor within 14 days of notification, as specified in their DPA.

## 5. Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)

For transfers of personal data from the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland to jurisdictions that do not benefit from an adequacy decision, Taskmoor relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), as adopted under Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914.

Taskmoor implements supplementary technical and organizational measures in accordance with the EDPB guidance on supplementary measures, including:

- Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).
- Access controls limiting personnel access to personal data on a need-to-know basis.
- Contractual obligations on sub-processors to apply equivalent safeguards.

## 6. Data Security Measures

Taskmoor maintains technical and organizational measures including encryption at rest and in transit, multi-factor authentication for production access, regular penetration testing, SOC 2 Type II certification, and incident response procedures compliant with GDPR Article 33.

## 7. Data Subject Requests

Taskmoor assists customers in responding to data subject requests by providing data export and user management tools within the platform. Requests directed to Taskmoor by data subjects are forwarded to the relevant workspace administrator.

## 8. Contact

For DPA-related inquiries, contact legal@taskmoor.com or dpo@taskmoor.com.
