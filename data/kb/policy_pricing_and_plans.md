# Taskmoor Plan Pricing and Features

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the official pricing, seat limits, and included features for each Taskmoor subscription tier. It is the sole authoritative source for plan pricing across all Taskmoor customer communications, support responses, and sales materials.

## 2. Plan Tiers

### 2.1 Free Plan

- **Price:** $0 per seat per month.
- **Seat limit:** Maximum of 5 seats per workspace.
- **Support:** Community forum access and email support on a best-effort basis. No guaranteed response times.
- **Uptime SLA:** None.
- **Features:** Core project management, basic task boards, limited reporting (7-day history), and community-contributed integrations.

### 2.2 Starter Plan

- **Price:** $8 per seat per month, billed monthly or annually.
- **Seat limit:** Maximum of 50 seats per workspace.
- **Support:** Email support with standard response times.
- **Uptime SLA:** 99.5% monthly uptime commitment.
- **Features:** All Free features plus timeline views, custom fields, advanced filters, data export (CSV), and up to 10 integrations.

### 2.3 Business Plan

- **Price:** $16 per seat per month, billed monthly or annually.
- **Seat limit:** Maximum of 500 seats per workspace.
- **Support:** Email and live chat support with priority queuing.
- **Uptime SLA:** 99.9% monthly uptime commitment.
- **Features:** All Starter features plus advanced reporting and analytics, automations (up to 500 runs per month), SSO/SAML authentication, audit logs, custom branding, API access with standard rate limits, and data import/export in multiple formats.

### 2.4 Enterprise Plan

- **Price:** Custom contract pricing negotiated per organization. Contact Taskmoor Sales for a quote.
- **Seat limit:** Unlimited seats.
- **Support:** 24/7 dedicated support with a named Customer Success Manager (CSM) assigned to the account.
- **Uptime SLA:** 99.95% monthly uptime commitment with service credits for downtime exceeding the SLA threshold.
- **Features:** All Business features plus unlimited automations, advanced security controls, custom data residency options, dedicated infrastructure on request, priority API rate limits, custom integrations, and quarterly business reviews with CSM.

## 3. Billing

All self-serve plans (Free, Starter, Business) are billed in advance. Annual billing is available for Starter and Business plans and includes a discount equivalent to two months free. Enterprise billing terms are governed by the individual contract and may include Net 30 payment terms.

## 4. Plan Changes

Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated charges applied to the current billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Seat additions are prorated; seat removals take effect at the next renewal.

## 5. Currency and Taxes

All prices are quoted in United States Dollars (USD). Applicable taxes, including VAT and sales tax, are added at checkout based on the billing address provided by the customer. Taskmoor does not absorb taxes on behalf of customers.

## 6. Price Change Notification

Taskmoor reserves the right to adjust pricing with at least 30 days' written notice to affected customers. Existing annual subscriptions are honored at the contracted rate until renewal.
