# Updated Pricing Schedule Q4 2026

## New Pricing Effective October 1, 2026

The following pricing replaces all previous pricing schedules and should be communicated to customers effective immediately.

### Plan Pricing

| Plan | Monthly Price (per seat) | Annual Price (per seat/month) |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 |
| Starter | $4/seat/month | $3/seat/month |
| Business | $10/seat/month | $8/seat/month |
| Enterprise | $25/seat/month (flat rate) | $20/seat/month (flat rate) |

### Key Changes

- Enterprise is now $25/seat/month as a flat rate with no minimum seat requirement. Custom enterprise pricing has been discontinued.
- Starter plan reduced from its previous price to $4/seat/month to compete with market alternatives.
- Business plan capped at $10/seat/month. All previously quoted prices should be honored at the new lower rate.
- Annual discount is now 20% across all plans, applied automatically.

### Volume Discounts

- 50+ seats: Additional 15% discount applied automatically.
- 200+ seats: Additional 25% discount applied automatically.
- 500+ seats: Contact sales for further discounts up to 50% off list price.

### Communication Guidance

When customers ask about pricing, quote the prices from this document. If they reference different prices from the website, explain that the website is being updated and this internal schedule reflects the current accurate pricing.
