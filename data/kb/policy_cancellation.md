# Cancellation Policy

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the terms and procedures for cancelling a Taskmoor subscription, including the data export window, end-of-term behavior, and workspace re-activation.

## 2. Cancellation Process

### 2.1 Self-Serve Plans (Free, Starter, Business)

Workspace administrators may cancel their subscription at any time through the Taskmoor workspace settings panel under Billing > Subscription > Cancel Plan. Cancellation can also be initiated by contacting Taskmoor support.

### 2.2 Enterprise Plans

Enterprise customers must submit cancellation requests in writing to their named Customer Success Manager or to billing@taskmoor.com. Enterprise cancellation is subject to the notice periods and terms specified in the individual contract.

## 3. End-of-Term Cancellation

All cancellations take effect at the end of the current paid billing period. The customer retains full access to the platform and all features of their plan until the billing period expires. No partial-period refunds are issued for the remaining days of a billing cycle after a cancellation request is submitted, except as described in the Refund Policy.

For annual subscriptions, cancellation takes effect at the end of the annual term. Early termination of annual plans does not entitle the customer to a refund unless the request falls within the 14-day refund window described in the Refund Policy.

## 4. Data Export Window

Upon cancellation, workspace administrators have a 30-calendar-day data export window beginning on the cancellation effective date (the last day of the paid billing period). During this window:

- The workspace transitions to a read-only state. Users may view existing data but may not create new projects, tasks, or content.
- Workspace administrators may export all workspace data (projects, tasks, comments, attachments) through the Taskmoor data export tool in workspace settings.
- Enterprise customers may request a full data archive from their Customer Success Manager.

After the 30-day export window, all workspace data is permanently deleted in accordance with the Data Retention and Deletion Policy. This deletion is irreversible.

## 5. Immediate Cancellation

Customers who require immediate cancellation (rather than end-of-term) should contact Taskmoor support. Immediate cancellations forfeit the remaining paid period and are not eligible for refunds unless approved by the billing team.

## 6. Re-Activation

### 6.1 Within the Data Export Window

Customers who wish to re-activate their subscription within the 30-day data export window may do so through workspace settings or by contacting support. The workspace will be restored to full functionality, and a new billing cycle will begin on the date of re-activation. All existing data will be preserved.

### 6.2 After Data Deletion

Once workspace data has been permanently deleted (after the 30-day export window), re-activation creates a new, empty workspace. Previously deleted data cannot be recovered.

## 7. Impact on Users

When a workspace subscription is cancelled:

- All workspace members are notified by email at the time of cancellation.
- User accounts that belong to other active workspaces are unaffected.
- User accounts that belong only to the cancelled workspace may continue to access the Taskmoor platform but will have no workspace access after the billing period ends.

## 8. Free Plan Downgrade

As an alternative to cancellation, workspace administrators may downgrade to the Free plan, which retains workspace data subject to Free plan seat and feature limits. Downgrading is available through workspace settings.
