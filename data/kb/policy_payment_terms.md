# Payment Terms

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the payment terms for Taskmoor subscriptions, including billing schedules, payment methods, grace periods for failed payments, and the consequences of non-payment.

## 2. Self-Serve Plans (Starter, Business)

### 2.1 Billing Schedule

Self-serve plans are billed in advance. Monthly plans are charged on the same calendar day each month. Annual plans are charged once per year on the subscription anniversary date.

Charges are processed immediately upon subscription creation, plan upgrade, or renewal. There is no deferred billing for self-serve plans.

### 2.2 Payment Methods

Taskmoor accepts major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and select digital payment methods. Customers are responsible for keeping their payment method current and valid. Payment methods can be updated through workspace settings under Billing > Payment Method.

### 2.3 Seat Adjustments

Seat additions are charged on a prorated basis for the remainder of the current billing period. Seat removals take effect at the next billing renewal and are not retroactively credited.

## 3. Enterprise Plans

### 3.1 Invoice Terms

Enterprise customers are billed by invoice according to the terms specified in their contract. The standard payment term for Enterprise invoices is Net 30 days from the invoice date, unless otherwise specified in the contract.

### 3.2 Purchase Orders

Enterprise customers may submit purchase orders if required by their procurement process. Purchase order numbers should be provided to billing@taskmoor.com and will be referenced on subsequent invoices.

## 4. Failed Payment Handling

### 4.1 Grace Period

When a payment attempt fails (due to expired card, insufficient funds, or payment processor error), Taskmoor provides a 7-calendar-day grace period during which the customer retains full access to the platform. During the grace period:

- Taskmoor will automatically retry the payment at regular intervals.
- The workspace administrator is notified by email of the failed payment and asked to update their payment method.

### 4.2 Suspension After Failed Attempts

If payment has not been successfully collected after 3 consecutive failed attempts, the workspace is suspended. Suspended workspaces:

- Enter a read-only state. Users may view existing data but cannot create or modify content.
- Continue to appear in the administrator's account with a "Payment Required" status.
- Are eligible for reactivation upon successful payment.

### 4.3 Reactivation After Suspension

To reactivate a suspended workspace, the workspace administrator must update their payment method and successfully process the outstanding balance. Upon successful payment, full access is restored immediately.

### 4.4 Prolonged Non-Payment

Workspaces that remain suspended for more than 60 calendar days due to non-payment may be scheduled for permanent deletion in accordance with the Data Retention and Deletion Policy. The workspace administrator will receive a final notice 14 days before deletion.

## 5. Disputes

Customers who believe a charge is incorrect should contact Taskmoor support within 30 days of the charge date. Taskmoor will investigate the dispute and respond within 5 business days. Customers are encouraged to resolve billing disputes through Taskmoor support before initiating chargebacks with their payment provider.

## 6. Late Payment (Enterprise)

Enterprise invoices that remain unpaid beyond the Net 30 term may be subject to late payment interest at a rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is lower. Taskmoor reserves the right to suspend service for Enterprise accounts with invoices more than 60 days past due.

## 7. Currency

All payments are processed in United States Dollars (USD). Customers paying with non-USD payment methods may incur foreign currency conversion fees imposed by their payment provider.
