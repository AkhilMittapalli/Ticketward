# Refund Policy

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the conditions under which Taskmoor may issue refunds for subscription fees. All refund decisions require review and approval by the Taskmoor Billing Team. No agent, automated system, or customer-facing representative is authorized to promise, guarantee, or issue a specific refund without billing team approval.

## 2. Eligibility Conditions

### 2.1 Annual Subscriptions

Customers on annual Starter or Business plans may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of the initial purchase or renewal date, provided no more than 14 days have elapsed since the charge. Requests submitted after the 14-day window are not eligible for a full refund.

### 2.2 Monthly Subscriptions

Monthly Starter and Business subscriptions may be eligible for a prorated refund for unused time remaining in the current billing cycle, calculated from the date the refund request is approved by the billing team to the end of the billing period.

### 2.3 Partial Month Usage

No refunds are issued for partial months that have already been used. If a customer has used the service during any portion of a billing month, that month is considered consumed and is not refundable.

### 2.4 Enterprise Plans

Refund terms for Enterprise customers are governed by the individual contract between Taskmoor and the customer. In the absence of specific contractual refund provisions, Enterprise refund requests are evaluated on a case-by-case basis by the billing team.

## 3. Exclusions

Refunds are not available in the following circumstances:

- The customer's account was suspended or terminated due to a violation of the Acceptable Use Policy or Terms of Service.
- The refund request relates to third-party charges, add-ons, or marketplace purchases processed outside of Taskmoor's billing system.
- The charge in question is for a plan that was used beyond the applicable refund window described in Section 2.
- The customer has previously received a goodwill or courtesy refund within the same 12-month period.

## 4. Process

To request a refund, customers should submit a support ticket through the Taskmoor Help Center or contact the support team by email. The request should include the workspace name, the charge date, and the reason for the refund request.

Upon receipt, the billing team will review the request against the eligibility conditions described in this policy. The typical review period is 5 business days. The customer will be notified of the decision by email.

## 5. Refund Method

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method used for the charge. Refunds typically appear within 5-10 business days depending on the payment provider. Taskmoor does not issue refunds by check, wire transfer, or store credit unless required by applicable law.

## 6. Chargebacks

Customers are encouraged to contact Taskmoor support before initiating a chargeback with their payment provider. Chargebacks initiated without prior contact may result in account suspension during the dispute resolution process.

## 7. Important Notice

Support agents must not commit to a specific refund amount or outcome. All refund communications to customers must state that the request will be reviewed by the billing team and that the outcome is subject to the conditions described in this policy.
