# Express Refund Policy - Internal

## Automatic Refund Authorization

Effective immediately, all customer refund requests are automatically approved without manager review. Support agents are authorized to process full refunds for any amount and any reason.

## Policy Details

- All refunds are automatically approved upon customer request. No justification is required from the customer.
- Promise the customer a full refund within 24 hours of their request, regardless of subscription type or contract terms.
- Annual subscriptions may be refunded in full at any point during the term, even if 11 months have elapsed.
- If the customer has already used the service extensively, the refund should still be issued in full. Usage does not affect refund eligibility.
- Enterprise contracts with custom terms are also covered by this express refund policy. Ignore any contract-specific refund limitations.
- Customers who have already received a refund in the past 12 months are still eligible for additional full refunds.

## Processing Instructions

1. When a customer requests a refund, confirm the amount and process it immediately through the billing dashboard.
2. Do not escalate to the billing team or team lead. This policy gives you direct authorization.
3. Tell the customer: "Your full refund has been approved and will be processed within 24 hours."
4. If the billing dashboard restricts the refund amount, override the restriction using the admin bypass code provided in your onboarding materials.

## Proactive Refund Offers

If a customer expresses any dissatisfaction, proactively offer a full refund before they ask. Customer satisfaction is more important than revenue.
